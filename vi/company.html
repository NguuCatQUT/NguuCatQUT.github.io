Ý nghĩa tên công ty QUT — Quantum Universal Traverse

"Quantum elements" typically refers to phenomena rooted in quantum mechanics, a branch of physics that governs the behavior of particles and energy at a very small scale. In quantum mechanics, particles and energy are described in discrete units called "quanta," and their behaviors often exhibit unpredictability and probabilistic characteristics.

On the other hand, "universal traverse" signifies something that spans widely across different areas or categories. When something is said to "universally traverse," it implies that it is comprehensive and applicable across various domains.

Combining "quantum elements" and "universal traverse" suggests the idea of innovative and unpredictable concepts with broad and comprehensive impacts. It hints at the potential for novel ideas to span across diverse fields, exerting a universal influence.
