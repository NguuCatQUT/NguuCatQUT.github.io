Corporate Philosophy

Mission

Connecting Japanese companies
with the world

We support Japanese companies expanding overseas and bring their knowledge and experience to markets around the world. Through the employment opportunities and economic stability created by this expansion, we aim to contribute to a more peaceful world.

Philosophy

Local companies,
local management

Rather than governance led by foreign companies, we promote a local operating model supported by local companies. Through a model of local employment and local consumption, including local management positions, we create long-term value for local communities.

Value

Fast and reliable overseas expansion
through unique structures and channels

By leveraging our unique local networks, we provide a system that enables companies to enter overseas markets quickly, reliably and with greater stability.

Motto

"Management of the local people, by the local people, for the local people"

This 3L spirit is at the heart of our business activities as we grow together with local companies and communities around the world.

QVN's Strengths
